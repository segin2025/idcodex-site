Readers, sales points and software

Readers on every lane accept QR and barcode, NFC, smart card, mobile ticket and biometric credentials. Ticket offices get touchscreen sales stations and thermal printers. Stadium management software shows live counts per gate, capacity by sector and every validation, so a closed lane is seen in the control room before the queue forms.

How a project runs.

The same four stages whether the venue seats 5,000 or 50,000, and whether we work for the federation, the contractor or the operator.

  1. Survey and design

    Gate counts from the capacity and the evacuation plan, lane types per entrance, cabling routes, power and network. Drawings in the contractor's format, documentation in English, French or Turkish.

  2. Supply and installation

    Hardware from our manufacturing partners, shipped and installed by our own site team alongside the main contractor's programme.

  3. Testing and commissioning

    Every lane load-tested with real tickets, integrated with the national ticketing provider, signed off with the federation's safety officer.

  4. Match day and maintenance

    Our engineers on site for the opening fixtures, then a local service partner with spare parts in the country, under a maintenance contract.
