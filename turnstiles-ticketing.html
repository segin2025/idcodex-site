Turnstiles and electronic ticketing

Secure entry, fast flow and one view of every gate. The hardware changes with the location; the ticket and the software stay the same.

What a stadium gate line needs.

A turnstile lets one authorised person through at a time, stops everyone else, and keeps the crowd moving in order. Ours read access cards, QR and barcode tickets, mobile tickets and biometric credentials, and every lane reports live to the stadium management software.

We size the gate line from the capacity and the evacuation plan: how many lanes per sector, which type at which entrance, where the step-free gates go. The same turnstiles are in use at stadiums from 9,000 to 50,000 seats in Uganda, Algeria, Georgia and Azerbaijan.

  • Full-height turnstile

    Floor-to-ceiling rotor for perimeters, unsupervised gates and high-security areas. No climbing over, single- or dual-direction, outdoor-rated with a long service life. Single and double versions.

  • Tripod turnstile

    The general-stands workhorse: compact, fast, low maintenance. Arms drop free on power loss or an evacuation signal. Angled or level cover.

  • Speed gate

    Glass wings and a silent motor for VIP, protocol, press and lobby entrances. Continuous, comfortable passage with card, QR, mobile ticket or facial recognition. Left/right and centre units.

  • Swing gate

    A wide, barrier-free lane for wheelchair users, pushchairs and visitors with reduced mobility. Sensor-guarded to prevent entrapment. Narrow and wide wing.

  • Readers

    Lane-mounted readers detect cards, QR and barcodes, mobile tickets and biometric data in milliseconds and pass the result to the access control system.

  • Stadium management software

    Ticketing, access control, turnstile status, parking, security and operations on one platform. Live counts per gate, capacity per sector and every validation, visible in the control room.

  • Sales station

    Touchscreen point-of-sale for tickets, products and services at the box office and inside the venue, working with the printers and payment systems.

  • Ticket printer

    Thermal printers for fast, legible, low-cost tickets at stadium and event entrances, integrated with the ticketing and sales systems.

Works with the ticketing platform already in use.

We integrate the gate line with the national or club ticketing provider rather than replacing it. Platforms connected so far: digiticket.dz (Algeria), biletebi.ge and TKT.ge (Georgia), iticket.az (Azerbaijan), mobicast.co.ug (Uganda).
