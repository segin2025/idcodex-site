Products

Everything a stadium or large venue needs between the car park and the pitch, supplied and integrated as one system.

  • Turnstiles and electronic ticketing

    Full-height, tripod, speed-gate and swing-gate lanes; readers for QR, NFC, smart card, mobile and biometric tickets; sales stations, ticket printers and stadium management software.

  • Smart parking

    Entry and exit terminals, barriers, automatic and manual payment stations, plate recognition and parking software for stadium and arena car parks.

  • Scoreboards and LED screens

    Match scoreboards, giant screens and perimeter LED for outdoor stadiums and indoor arenas, with broadcast and VAR integration. Manufactured by TAGLIG.

  • Smart card and biometric ID

    Access and season cards, national ID and driver-licence programmes, biometric verification and the card personalisation systems behind them.

  • CCTV and low-voltage infrastructure

    Fixed and PTZ cameras, video management and analytics, network and fibre, cabling, distribution panels, generators and emergency lighting.

  • VR game areas

    Turnkey virtual-reality game areas from 30 to 200 square metres for entertainment centres and shopping malls.
