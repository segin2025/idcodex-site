CCTV and low-voltage infrastructure

Cameras, network, power and cabling for the whole venue, installed by the same team that installs the gates.

One contractor for everything behind the wall.

A gate line is only as reliable as the cable, the switch and the generator behind it. We take the low-voltage and electrical scope together with the systems that depend on it, from procurement and customs to installation, testing and commissioning under one point of responsibility.

Delivered at Ganja City Stadium (electrical infrastructure and generators) and at Divan Suites Batumi and Mercure Old Town Tbilisi (electrical, low-voltage and IT).

  • CCTV

    High-resolution fixed and PTZ cameras, video management and recording, AI-based analytics and a central monitoring room.

  • Network and cabling

    Fibre and copper network, cable trays and structured cabling for stadiums and hotels.

  • Electrical distribution

    Distribution panels, emergency lighting and the power design for match-day load.

  • Generator backup

    Standby generation sized for the venue, so the gates and screens stay up when the grid does not.
