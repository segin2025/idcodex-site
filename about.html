About idCodeX

An international systems integrator for stadiums, large venues and public projects, built on Barkodes engineering.

Who we are

idCodeX Technology Solutions designs, installs and commissions ticketing, access control, smart parking, scoreboard and LED systems for live operation, and delivers the IT, card and automation systems that organisations of every size depend on.

Integrated systems from idCodeX run in stadiums of up to 53,000 seats in Africa, the CIS and the MENA region, serving millions of spectators a season. The company is the international brand of Barkodes engineering, Turkey, and works with national integrators and service partners in each country.

Our staff have held management positions on major investment projects and bring consultancy, outsourcing, equipment, testing, software development and system continuity services to clients in their own field.

Sustainability

Our projects follow the sustainability pillars used in European football: anti-racism, child and youth protection, equality and inclusion, sport for all abilities, health and well-being, solidarity and rights, circular economy, climate, event sustainability and infrastructure sustainability.
