Ticketing

Electronic ticketing providers and stadium integration

Hosting the match is not enough any more. Fan experience, security and operating cost are judged just as hard, and electronic ticketing sits at the centre of all three. idCodeX integrates the ticketing platform a club or federation already uses with the stadium's gates, screens and security systems, so the venue is run from one place.

1. What electronic ticketing gives a stadium

  • Fast, secure entry without long queues
  • Sales, cancellations and reissues managed centrally
  • Fan data: demographics and behaviour for marketing and operations
  • Single-use QR or NFC tickets that cannot be copied

2. Why the integration matters

  • Gates and turnstiles validate tickets in real time
  • Scoreboards and screens can carry wayfinding or sponsor messages in sync
  • Security systems support a fast, controlled evacuation
  • Fans check ticket and seat status in the mobile or web app

3. How idCodeX integrates

  • Several ticket vendors managed through one central system
  • Turnstile and access control with live verification
  • Live reporting on sales, entries, exits and occupancy
  • Infrastructure that accepts new technologies and campaigns without a rebuild

4. Operational and commercial results

  • Faster, safer entry and higher fan satisfaction
  • One data set for the whole operation
  • Advertising and promotion space used more effectively
  • Ticket fraud reduced to a minimum

Platforms we have connected

digiticket.dz in Algeria, biletebi.ge and TKT.ge in Georgia, iticket.az in Azerbaijan and mobicast.co.ug in Uganda, each tied to the gate line we installed. Integration of the ticketing platform with the stadium infrastructure is what makes a modern venue run centrally, securely and efficiently; it is the part of the project we take responsibility for.
