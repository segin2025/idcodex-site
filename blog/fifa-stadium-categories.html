FIFA stadium categories: what each level requires

1. Category 1 Stadiums

Category 1 includes stadiums that meet the basic requirements for lower-level international matches or smaller tournaments. They include:

Basic locker rooms

Limited media areas

Minimum lighting requirements

2. Category 2 Stadiums

Category 2 stadiums represent a higher level and can host regional tournaments and mid-level international fixtures. Additional requirements:

Enhanced lighting

Larger media working areas

Minimum seating capacity

Improved security systems

3. Category 3 Stadiums

These are modern stadiums capable of hosting significant international matches. Requirements include:

  • Minimum 10,000 fully seated capacity
  • Expanded VIP areas
  • Advanced broadcasting booths
  • Professional team preparation rooms
  • High-level turf maintenance standards

4. Category 4 Stadiums (Top Level)

The highest FIFA category, suitable for events such as the FIFA World Cup and major finals. Key requirements:

  • Minimum 20,000 all-seater capacity
  • Excellent sightlines
  • Large media centers, TV studios, and commentary booths
  • VIP, VVIP, and protocol zones
  • Advanced security and emergency facilities
  • High-quality lighting and audio systems
  • Modern electronic ticketing and access control systems
