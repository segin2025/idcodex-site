Smart parking

Entry in under two seconds, payment without a queue, and a car park that reports to the same control room as the gates.

From the barrier to the receipt.

Stadium car parks fill in an hour and empty in twenty minutes. We design the lanes, the terminals and the payment points for that peak, and connect them to plate recognition and parking management software so that occupancy, revenue and faults are visible live.

Delivered at Tizi Ouzou Stadium in Algeria as part of the stadium's access package.

  • Entry terminal

    Issues a ticket in under two seconds without a button press. Reads contactless subscriber cards and modern ticket types; optional hardware modules.

  • Barriers

    Fast opening and closing, advanced safety features and a modern housing for lanes with heavy traffic.

  • Automatic payment terminal

    Contactless, cash and card payment integrated with plate recognition and the parking software, cutting waiting time at the exit.

  • Card payment terminal

    Card and contactless payment with a simple interface usable by all age groups, available around the clock.

  • Manual payment station

    A staffed payment point with card and contactless support for attended car parks and events.
