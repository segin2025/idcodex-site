Scoreboards and LED screens

Scoreboards, giant screens and perimeter LED that read clearly from the top row and on television.

Built for outdoor light and broadcast cameras.

A stadium screen has to be legible in afternoon sun, flicker-free on camera and still working after ten seasons of rain. We supply LED hardware manufactured by TAGLIG and integrate it with the scoring, broadcast, VAR and stadium management systems, then hand over a control room that one operator can run.

Delivered at Boris Paichadze Dinamo Arena (53,279 seats) in Tbilisi and Independence Stadium in Bakau, The Gambia.

  • Scoreboard

    Score, time, periods, team names, player information and statistics in real time. High brightness and resolution, indoor and outdoor housings, remote control software, integration with broadcast and VAR.

  • Giant screen

    Live feed, replays, graphics, animations and advertising at high resolution and brightness, with wide viewing angles for every seat.

  • Perimeter LED

    Pitch-side advertising and information boards with a high refresh rate for flicker-free television pictures; impact-resistant and weatherproof.

Choosing a scoreboard

Pixel pitch, brightness, refresh rate, viewing distance and service access are the five numbers that decide whether a screen works in a stadium. We wrote them up in a short guide.
